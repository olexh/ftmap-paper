# corpus-external — provenance

Public, openly published tabular sources, downloaded over the public internet
with no login and kept as downloaded. The etalons in
`docs/measurements/etalon/` are written against these files. Nothing here may
be tuned to improve a number.

`person-graph/` holds Ukrainian registers where a natural person appears and a
relation exists. `shape-stress/` holds two files kept for their format defects
(padded sheets, hidden legend sheet, two-row headers, date serials, column
shear), not their content.

Licence is Creative Commons Attribution as recorded on the dataset page unless
stated. Retrieved 2026-08-20 unless stated.

## Slice rules

Three files are slices of sources too large to ship. Each keeps the header
and every n-th data row in source order, with values untouched.

* `ua_tax_debtors_2022-02-23_every40th.csv`: `borg.csv` from the ZIP, 861 128
  data rows; every 40th row kept, 21 529 rows × 14 columns. Rewritten through
  Python's `csv` module with `QUOTE_ALL`, so quoting is normalised; field
  order and values are not.
* `ua_court_decisions_2026_every250th.csv`: `documents.csv` from the ZIP,
  5 951 608 data rows; every 250th row kept.
* `ua_enforcement_debtors_2026-09-02_every200th.csv`: the CSV from the ZIP
  dated 2026-09-02; every 200th row kept, 52 737 rows × 11 columns,
  Windows-1251, semicolon delimited, bytes untouched.
